# Third-party content

Ashcombe Hollow is built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT licence, see `vendor/three/LICENSE`).

The 3D models under `assets/` are released by their authors under the
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) public-domain dedication.
Attribution is not required by the licence; it is given here with thanks.

| Folder | Pack | Author | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/kaykit-halloween-bits/` | KayKit — Halloween Bits 1.0 | Kay Lousberg, [kaylousberg.com](https://kaylousberg.com) | CC0 (`LICENSE.txt` in the folder) |
| `assets/kenney/graveyard/` | Graveyard Kit | Kenney, [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl) | CC0 (`assets/kenney/LICENSE-kenney.txt`) |
| `assets/kenney/nature/` | Nature Kit | Kenney, [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl) | CC0 |
| `assets/kenney/survival/` | Survival Kit | Kenney, [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl) | CC0 |

The Kenney kits were obtained from the [shorepine/kenney](https://github.com/shorepine/kenney) glTF mirror of Kenney's
all-in-one asset bundle; the KayKit pack from [KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Halloween-Bits-1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Halloween-Bits-1.0).
Only the models the game uses were copied. Everything else in the game (textures, buildings, terrain, audio) is generated in code.

Typography is loaded from Google Fonts (IM Fell English, Caveat) under the SIL Open Font License, with system fallbacks.
